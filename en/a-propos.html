Who is ROLF?

A passion for watchmaking and jewellery, a brand born in France

Our philosophy

Much more than a watch or a piece of jewellery

ROLF was born from a simple conviction: a fine watch or piece of jewellery shouldn't be chosen at random.

We select renowned brands — from AVI-8's aviation heritage to the Swiss elegance of Jacques du Manoir, from Jackie Gold's 14K gold jewellery to Orus's creations — to bring together, in one place, pieces with character, at fair prices.

Every reference is chosen for its quality, its story and its ability to accompany the moments that matter.
