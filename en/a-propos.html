Who is ROLF?

A passion for watchmaking and jewellery, a brand born in France

Our philosophy

Much more than a watch or a piece of jewellery

At ROLF, we believe every watch and every piece of jewellery is far more than a simple accessory — they are everyday pieces that accompany your most precious moments, without ever weighing on your budget.

ROLF is the first name of its founder, Florian — a short name, simple to write, that reflects who we are: direct, no fuss.

Since our founding, we have carefully selected every model, watch and jewellery alike, to offer you the best value for money, from our workshop in Bruz, France.

Our journey

A wonderful adventure since 2016

2016
The beginning

Florian starts the brand in his hometown of Bruz with a clear vision: make quality watchmaking accessible to everyone.

2018
First online store

The website launches and we ship our first orders across France. The community grows quickly.

2020
International expansion

The brand starts shipping across Europe and launches its first "Géométrique" collection, which goes on to become iconic.

2022
200+ models

The catalogue passes 200 models with new Vintage, Swiss and Children's ranges. 4.8/5 on Avis Vérifiés.

2026
Watches & jewellery, a new era

The brand becomes ROLF: a complete website redesign, an expansion into jewellery (rings, necklaces, bracelets) and the launch of our loyalty programme.
