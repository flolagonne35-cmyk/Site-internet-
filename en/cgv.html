3. Orders

An order is confirmed once the basket is validated, delivery details are entered and payment is made. A confirmation email is sent to the address provided. ROLF reserves the right to cancel or refuse any order in the event of a prior dispute or a stock shortage.

4. Payment

Payment is due immediately upon ordering. Accepted payment methods are: credit/debit card, PayPal and instalment payment (subject to amount and eligibility). Transactions are secured by encryption.

5. Delivery

Orders are shipped within France within 2 to 4 business days. Delivery is free from €80 of purchase; below that threshold, costs are shown before the order is confirmed. A tracking number is sent by email and SMS as soon as the order is shipped.

6. Right of withdrawal (statutory 14 days)

In accordance with articles L.221-18 et seq. of the French Consumer Code, you have a statutory period of 14 days from receipt of your order to exercise your right of withdrawal, without having to give a reason.

7. Commercial returns policy (30 days)

Beyond the statutory period, ROLF extends this to 30 days as a commercial goodwill policy: you may return an unworn item, in its original packaging, for an exchange or refund. Full details of the procedure are available on our Shipping & Returns page.

8. Warranty

All products carry a 2-year manufacturer's warranty against manufacturing defects, in addition to the statutory warranty of conformity (articles L.217-3 et seq. of the French Consumer Code) and the warranty against hidden defects (article 1641 of the French Civil Code).

9. Personalisation and engraving

Personalised items (engraving) are not eligible for the right of withdrawal once engraving has been carried out, in accordance with article L.221-28 of the French Consumer Code relating to goods made to the consumer's specifications. The engraving turnaround time is always communicated before the order is confirmed.

The terms of the loyalty programme and the referral programme are detailed on the dedicated page. ROLF reserves the right to change these programmes; points already earned remain valid.

11. Mediation

If a dispute cannot be resolved directly with our customer service, you may refer it free of charge to a consumer mediator, in accordance with article L.616-1 of the French Consumer Code.
