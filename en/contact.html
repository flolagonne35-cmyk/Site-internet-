Frequently asked questions

FAQ

Answers to the questions our customers ask most.

What is your return policy? +
You have 30 days after receiving your watch to return it free of charge if it doesn't suit you. The watch must be in its original condition with packaging.
How long does delivery take? +
Standard delivery in France takes 2 to 4 business days. Express delivery is available within 24h. Delivery is free from €80 spent.
What warranty applies to watches? +
All our watches come with a 2-year manufacturer warranty covering manufacturing defects. After-sales service is handled by our team in Bruz.
Do you ship internationally? +
Yes, we ship across Europe and beyond. International shipping costs and delivery times vary by destination. Contact us for more information.
How can I track my order? +
A confirmation email with a tracking link is sent as soon as your parcel ships. You can also contact us directly for a personal update.
Can I have a watch engraved? +
Yes! We offer a personalised engraving service on certain models. Contact us before placing your order to check eligible models and pricing.
