Engagement

The right piece of jewellery for a yes that changes everything

A proposal takes planning — and the ring is often its most lasting symbol. At ROLF, every engagement ring is delivered with its certificate of authenticity and free gift wrapping, so that only the moment matters.

How to choose well

Size — discreetly borrow a ring already worn to measure it, or choose an adjustable style.
Material — gold, silver or steel: all our rings state the hallmark and the traceability of the stones.
Engraving — available on many models, with a turnaround of 3 to 5 business days to plan for.
Returns — 30 days to exchange the size if needed, free of charge.

Our pick

The Jackie Gold Impala Ring, in 14-carat gold, is an understated, timeless style that's a firm favourite for proposals.
