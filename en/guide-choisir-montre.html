How to choose your watch?

The complete guide before you buy

Between the case, the strap and the movement, choosing a watch can quickly get complicated. Here are the essential points to check before buying, whether for yourself or as a gift.

1. The movement

Quartz: battery-powered, accurate, low-maintenance — ideal for everyday use.
Automatic: wound by the motion of the wrist, no battery needed, prized for its traditional mechanical character.

2. The case

  • Material: stainless steel (hard-wearing, affordable), gold-plated (elegance at a lower price), titanium (lightweight, premium).
  • Shape: round (timeless), square or rectangular (more distinctive).
  • Diameter: 38–42mm on average for men, 28–34mm for women — adjust to suit wrist size.

3. Water resistance

Shown in ATM or metres: 3 ATM withstands splashes, 5 ATM an occasional shower, 10 ATM and above swimming. For diving, look for at least 20 ATM.

4. The strap

Leather: dressy look, best avoided with heavy sweating or frequent contact with water.
Steel: durable, easy to adjust, versatile.
Silicone: comfortable, resistant to water and sweat — perfect for sport or for children.

5. Warranty and after-sales service

Check the warranty period (2 years at ROLF) and how easy after-sales service is. An official retailer guarantees genuine parts and support if something goes wrong.
