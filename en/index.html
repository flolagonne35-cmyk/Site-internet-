Our story

Founded by Florian, since 2016

ROLF was born from a simple conviction: every moment deserves to be celebrated in style, without emptying your wallet. Founded by Florian, the brand curates multi-brand watches and jewellery with a single criterion — real value for money.

Based in Bruz, France, ROLF carefully selects every piece in its catalogue to bring you the best of accessible watchmaking and jewellery craft.

Certified pieces

Watches and jewellery tested, hallmarked and guaranteed.

Responsible materials
