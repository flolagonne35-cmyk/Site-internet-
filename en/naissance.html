New baby

A first piece of jewellery, for a lifetime

Giving a piece of jewellery for a new baby means giving a keepsake for life. Designed with grandparents, godparents and new parents in mind, our selection favours understated pieces in silver or gold plate, often available engraved with a first name or date of birth.

Our tips

Gentle materials — silver or gold, hypoallergenic, for baby-soft skin or a piece kept safely in its box.
Engraved first name — on a pendant or ID bracelet, a personalised keepsake (3-5 day turnaround).
Keepsake box — free gift wrapping, perfect to go along with a birth announcement.
For the parents too — a piece of jewellery or a watch to mark their new role.
