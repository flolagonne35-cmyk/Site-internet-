Shipping & returns

Simple, free, no bad surprises

Delivery

Free delivery in France from €80 of purchase (€4.90 below that threshold). Average delivery time of 2 to 4 business days. A tracking email followed by an SMS is sent as soon as your order ships, with a real-time tracking link.

Delivery across Europe is available — costs and timeframes are calculated at checkout depending on the destination.

Returning an item

You have 30 days after receipt to change your mind (statutory withdrawal period: 14 days, extended by ROLF to 30 days). The item must be unworn, in its original packaging, with the invoice included.

1

Request a return

From your customer account or by email at contact@rolf.fr, within 30 days.

2

Get your label

A prepaid return label is emailed to you, to print or use as a QR code.

3

Ship the parcel

Drop the parcel off at a collection point. No charge for a standard return within France.

4

Refund

Within 5 business days after the item is received and inspected, to your original payment method.

Special cases

Engraved / personalised items: not returnable once engraving has been carried out, except in the case of a manufacturing defect — the engraving turnaround time is always communicated before the order is confirmed.

Size exchange: also free, just mention it when requesting your return.

Warranty

2-year manufacturer's warranty on watches and 1-year on jewellery, covering manufacturing defects (movement, clasp, plating). Contact our customer service with your order number for any claim.

Have questions? Our team replies within 24h via the Contact page.
